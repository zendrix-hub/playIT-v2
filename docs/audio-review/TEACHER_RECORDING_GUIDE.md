# Recording the 13 short sounds (for the teacher)

Thank you for helping. playIT needs clean recordings of 13 letter-sounds that a computer voice can't make correctly. The app plays them to Grade 1 children, so each sound must be pure: no vowel after it.

## What to record
b, c, d, g, h, j, k, p, q, t, w, x, y

| Letter | Sound | Say it like the start of… | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| b | /b/ | ball | "buh", "bee" |
| c | /k/ | cat | "kuh", "see" |
| d | /d/ | dog | "duh", "dee" |
| g | /g/ | goat | "guh", "jee" |
| h | /h/ | hat (a short breath) | "huh" |
| j | /dʒ/ | jug | "juh", "jay" |
| k | /k/ | kite | "kuh", "kay" |
| p | /p/ | pig (a small puff of air) | "puh", "pee" |
| q | /kw/ | queen | "kyoo" |
| t | /t/ | tiger (a small puff of air) | "tuh", "tee" |
| w | /w/ | watch (short, lips round) | "wuh", "double-u" |
| x | /ks/ | the end of box | "eks" |
| y | /j/ | yoyo (short) | "wai", "yuh" |

Tip: say the word, then say it again but stop before the vowel. "B-all" becomes just the /b/.

## How to record
1. Use a phone's voice recorder app in a quiet room. Turn off fans and aircon if you can.
2. Hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth, slightly to the side.
3. For each letter, say its name once ("b"), wait a second, then say the sound **3 times** with a 2-second pause between each.
4. One file per letter is fine. Name it with the letter, for example `b.m4a`.
5. Send the 13 files to the team.

It takes about 10 minutes. The team trims the files and checks them, and you'll be asked to approve the final versions (Gate 3).
