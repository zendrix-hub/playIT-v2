## Form Title:
### **PlayIT: Early Literacy Mobile App — MVP User Validation & Evaluation (UPA Framework)**

## Form Description:
Thank you for participating in the user validation of **PlayIT**, an offline-first Android mobile application designed to teach Grade 1 English phonics using the Department of Education (DepEd) Marungko Approach.

This instrument evaluates the application across three core pedagogical and design pillars:
1. **Usability (U):** Ease of navigation, child engagement, and standardized caregiver usability (Smileometer + SUS).
2. **Pedagogy (P):** DepEd Marungko instructional sequence fidelity, phonetic accuracy, and empirical reading gain (Pre/Post Test).
3. **Accessibility (A):** Pediatric ergonomics (≥64dp touch targets), non-verbal audio scaffolding, and 100% offline zero-data equity.

Your responses are confidential and will be analyzed exclusively for IT411 Capstone Research.
