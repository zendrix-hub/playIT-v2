## Form Title:
### **PlayIT: Early Literacy Mobile App — MVP User Validation & Evaluation**

## Form Description:
Thank you for participating in the user validation of **PlayIT**, an offline-first Android mobile application designed to teach Grade 1 English phonics using the Department of Education (DepEd) Marungko Approach. 

This survey collects feedback to evaluate the usability, pedagogical appropriateness, and overall user acceptance of our Minimum Viable Product (MVP). Your responses are confidential and will be used exclusively for research and academic evaluation in IT411 (Capstone & Research 2).
