## For the documents (Zendrix)

The SRS, SDD and STD describe the earlier behaviour. These parts change:

- **FR-10, FR-12 (Parent Zone, PDF report):**
  - Mastered/Developing/At-Risk and percentages become plain words for parents; the DepEd descriptors and transmuted grades are in the PDF only, for teachers.
  - The Parent Zone shows a row per child, the child's summary with "Practice next", and the 26 letters as a grid with details on a tap (no per-letter text).
  - The PDF is for the facilitator, teacher first: it covers every child on the phone, starts each child with "Needs help with", lists only started chapters, and has no lines to fill in.
  - A replay keeps the best stars.
- **FR-04 (Find It):** words under the pictures depend on the chapter and on replays: every word in Chapter 1 (also on replays), two in Chapter 2, none from Chapter 3 or on a replay from Chapter 2 on.
- **Lily's design (character guide, every screenshot of her):** the user's new Lily of 2026-11-07 replaces the 2026-10-12 one.
- **FR-03 (Say It):** the word-mode grammar adds near-miss decoys, so a wrong first sound is not accepted.
- **FR-13 (Blend It):** each word game opens with a sound check; FR-10 and FR-12 show its first and latest result per chapter.
- **NFR-ACC-01 (mouth-shape cues):** the cue shows big while Lily models the sound twice, and the mouth pulses while the sound plays; then it moves beside the mic.
- **NFR-IND-01 (independent use):** the return greeting, the child-language wording, the Guidebook content, and the name a new player types (replacing avatar-only onboarding).
- **STD:** the new tests are `ProgressLevelTest`, `ProgressRecordCalculatorTest`, `ReportGeneratorTest`, `ReportLayoutTest`, `ReportPageRendererTest`, `BestResultTest`, `WordLabelPolicyTest`, `ReturnGreetingPolicyTest`, `ChildWordsTest`, `ParentDashboardScreenshotTest`, `ParentZoneColorsTest`, `PracticeNextTest` and `WelcomeBackScreenshotTest`, plus the updated view-model and layout tests. The third round adds `SoundCheckRoundTest`, `SoundCheckRepositoryImplTest`, `SoundCheckViewModelTest` and `SoundCheckScreenshotTest`, and decoy tests in `SpeechValidatorTest`.

No database schema changed. A teacher audit of the new clips is still required before merging to main.
