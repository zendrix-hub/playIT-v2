# Research Poster and Two-Minute Video Pitch
## TekCon 2026 - Capstone & SoftEng Research Conference
### IT411 | G1 - G8 | SEM1 | 2026-27 |
 
As part of your Capstone 2 and Software Engineering 2 requirements, all teams are required to prepare and submit a Research Poster and a Two-Minute Video Pitch for TekCon 2026.
 
These outputs will showcase your completed project, its technical implementation, validation results, and potential impact.
 
## Required Deliverables

### 1. Research Poster
Each team must prepare a research poster using the required physical cartolina paper size.
Your poster should communicate your project through visual storytelling rather than simply presenting lengthy paragraphs or copying sections from your research paper.

Include the following key information:
- **Problem**: What specific problem or need does your project address?
- **Solution**: What is your proposed system or innovation?
- **How It Works**: Present the system architecture, workflow, key features, or screenshots.
- **Results and Validation**: Highlight actual testing results, user feedback, and measurable outcomes.
- **Impact**: Explain the value and potential contribution of your project.
- **Team Information**: Include the project title and team members.
- **Selected References**: Include relevant references where appropriate.
- **Mandatory QR Code**: Your poster must contain a QR code that links directly to your team's two-minute video pitch uploaded to Google Drive.

Use the label:
> "Scan to Watch Our 2-Minute Project Pitch"

Make sure that:
- The video is uploaded to Google Drive.
- The sharing permission is set to Anyone with the link – Viewer, subject to university sharing policies.
- The QR code directs users to the correct video.
- The QR code is tested before printing.

#### Poster Design Reminders:
- Use concise text and a clear visual hierarchy.
- Include relevant diagrams, screenshots, infographics, and illustrations.
- Highlight actual results and measurable outcomes.
- Maintain readability, proper spacing, and a balanced layout.
- Make your poster visually engaging and professionally presented.

There will be a Best Poster Award during TekCon 2026. Posters will be appreciated not only for their visual appeal but also for how effectively they communicate the problem, solution, technical work, results, and significance of the project.
 
---

### 2. Two-Minute Video Pitch
Each team must produce a video pitch with a maximum running time of two minutes.
The video should briefly communicate the value of your project and demonstrate the actual system.

#### Suggested presentation flow:
| Time | Content |
|---|---|
| **0:00–0:20** | **Problem** – Introduce the problem or need. |
| **0:20–0:40** | **Solution** – Present your proposed system. |
| **0:40–1:10** | **Demonstration** – Show how the system works. |
| **1:10–1:40** | **Results and Validation** – Highlight actual results and evidence. |
| **1:40–2:00** | **Impact and Closing** – Explain the project's value and potential impact. |
 
Keep the narration clear and concise. Use actual screen recordings or demonstrations of your working system. Ensure that the audio is understandable and that the video is professionally edited.
